Staging
No curtain in the front
Musicians visible most of the time

Costume
Plain black muslin hat with wings shows ruler
Thick black beard for *jing* roles who are military leaders
Jade belts used to indicate status of the nobles 
Red indicates nobility. Only the emperor would be allowed to have his robe with dragons on
Thick-soled boots are common
Certain expressions 

Make up 
Red = positive brave 
Purple = wisdom and brave 
Black = Neutral, brave
Blue and Green = Neutral, Hero of the bush 
Yellow and White = Negative, treacherous, crafty
Gold and Silver = Mysteriousness, monsters, gods
