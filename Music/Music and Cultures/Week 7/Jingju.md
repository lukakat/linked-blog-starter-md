Also called Beijing opera or Peking opera 

Dynastic periods
- [[Ming Dynasty]] (1368 to 1644)
- [[Quing Dynastsy]] (1644 to 1911)
- Republic of China (1911 to 1949)

Developed in Beijin during the [[Quing Dynastsy]] 
Boxer rebellion and poverty period 
Golden age in 20th century and put in national theatre

History
Troupes moved to Beijing bringing traditions with them

Parents loaned their sons to theatre troupes to get money
Stopped after Taiping rebellion from 1850 to 1864
