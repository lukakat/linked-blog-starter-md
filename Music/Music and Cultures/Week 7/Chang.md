Highly stylised ways of making melody, including other sounds like crying or laughter  
• Each role has a specific voice type  
• Dan: high falsetto; melody can be stepwise and somewhat fluid  
• Jing: ‘thunderous’ quality, range can be quite varied; melody is not often stepwise and  
fluid; laughter is especially stylised here  
• Example from Farewell My Concubine (the Opera): 15:05- 17:10
