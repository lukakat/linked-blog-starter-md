[[Vedas]]
Texts that appeared during the [[Aryans]] (1700 to 500 BC)
- poetic descriptions of gods and nature 
- Chanted from memory 
- Single unaccompanied vocal line 
- Syllable is central focus of sound 
-  Chant is syllabic 

Each syllable is ornamented with a note above and below later forming fundamental indian classical music

### Patronage

salons hired courtesan musicians and accompanying musicians for performances 
most musicians are from muslim families who studied within the family, called "hereditary musicians"
