Summary
==
Efforts aim to recreate period sound using original instruments, techniques, sources, and score-based decisions. Performers try to reflect composer intentions and give audiences a period-style experience.

Main issues: styles and conventions shift over time, evidence is incomplete, and accuracy is uncertain. Claims of authenticity create disputes. Marketing language upset modern performers.

Critics argue performers select historical elements that suit modern taste. The result reflects present values more than past practice. Taruskin, Kenyon, Bilson, and Gardiner appear often in these debates.
